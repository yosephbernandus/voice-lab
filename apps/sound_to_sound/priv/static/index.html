THE VOICE JOURNEY

One sound, one path.

Follow the stages. Device hardware sits outside this page.

  1. 01DEVICE HARDWARE · OUTSIDE JAVASCRIPTAir pressure → microphone → ADC

    Pressure moves a microphone membrane; the device samples its voltage.

  2. 02BROWSER + LAB JAVASCRIPTDigital PCM → low-pass → downsample to 8 kHz → μ-law

    The browser holds samples; the lab filters, resamples, and encodes each frame.

  3. 03RELAY (ELIXIR) + RECEIVER (BROWSER)Packets → relay → receiver buffer → decode

    Frames carry identity and timing before the listener gets them.

  4. 04DEVICE HARDWARE · OUTSIDE JAVASCRIPTDAC → output circuit → speaker → air

    The receiving device turns numbers back into pressure.
